QuranMotion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short: QuranMotion has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. When you use hand or eye control, the camera picture is processed only on your phone. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Who we are

QuranMotion is a free Quran reading app, available at quranmotion.com and as an app for Android. It was made by Osman Hashi.

Information we collect

None. QuranMotion does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal details, and it has no sign in.

Information kept on your device

To remember your reading, the app saves a few settings in your phone's own storage: the page you last read, your bookmarks, pages you read recently, your chosen translation, text size, page colour and hands-free settings. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it.

Camera

The camera is only used for hand gestures and for eye scroll. When they are on:

  • The camera picture is analysed on your phone to find the shape of your hand, or whether your eyes are looking at the top or bottom of the screen.
  • No photos or videos are recorded or saved, and nothing from the camera is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your face is not shown on screen unless you choose "Show camera picture".
  • The camera turns off as soon as you stop hands-free mode or leave the reader.

You can refuse or remove camera permission at any time in your phone's settings. The rest of the app works without it.

Microphone (voice commands)

The microphone is only used if you tap the microphone button to switch on voice commands (such as "next", "back", "up", "down", "surah 36" or "page 250"). Speech recognition is done by your browser: in Chrome the speech is sent to Google to turn it into words, and in Safari to Apple. QuranMotion only looks for those four commands, and does not record, save or send your voice anywhere itself. Tap the button again to stop listening.

Motion sensor

If you switch on Tilt the phone, the app reads your phone's tilt to turn pages. This stays on your device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Internet connections

The Arabic Quran and English translation are built into the app and work offline. The app connects to these services only when needed:

  • alquran.cloud, to load translations in other languages when you choose them.
  • Google Fonts, for some of the app's lettering.
  • jsDelivr, to download the Madinah Quran script (King Fahd Complex font) the first time you open each part of the Quran, and the hand tracking tool.
  • Google storage, to download the hand tracking model the first time you use it.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts quranmotion.com.

Like any website, these services receive basic technical information such as your IP address so they can send the files. QuranMotion does not send them anything about you or your reading. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Children

QuranMotion is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
